## Source discipline

Verification uses information published by 17 June 2026. Later-filed FY25 accounts are not substituted. The £3.5bn debt figure is the September-2025 public anchor; exact June-2026 tranche balances, covenant definitions, security/guarantor terms, loan margins, hedges and commitment-fee rates are not independently verified. Exchange listings verify instrument terms but are not complete credit agreements.

Public values are separated from assumptions in the workbook. Forecasts, GBP translation, cash immediately before the proposed refinancing, the illustrative £5m of other transaction fees, pricing, covenants and the credit conclusion are the author's work and are not statements by Morrisons or any lender.
