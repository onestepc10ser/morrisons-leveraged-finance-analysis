# Methodology

## 1. Scope

This is a lender underwriting model for a refinancing, not an equity LBO returns model. The central questions are whether Morrisons can service and repay the debt, how the answer changes under stress, whether committed liquidity is sufficient and which contractual protections a lender should require.

The model uses a simplified cash-flow approach because public disclosure does not support a reliable full three-statement forecast. It prioritises EBITDA, cash interest, capital expenditure, lease principal, working-capital movement, tax, cash sweep and RCF mechanics.

## 2. Public facts and independent assumptions

Public facts are identified in green in the workbook and linked to the source register. The principal disclosed anchors are:

- FY2024/25 revenue of £15.8bn and underlying EBITDA of £835m.
- Approximately £3.5bn of gross debt following the 2025 refinancing.
- A £1.0bn committed RCF.
- 2025 refinancing instruments including £500m and €500m secured notes due 2031.
- A residual secured-note balance due 2027 and £750m senior notes due 2028.

Forecast growth, margins, cash conversion, rates, opening cash, exchange-rate translation and covenant levels are independent analytical assumptions. They should not be read as management guidance.

## 3. Transaction sizing

The hypothetical facility is rounded to **£900m**:

- approximately **£146m equivalent** of residual 2027 secured notes, comprising £20.533m and €145.696m of principal translated into GBP;
- **£750m** of 2028 senior notes; and
- modest room for transaction fees, foreign-exchange movement and rounding.

The facility is maturity-neutral rather than deleveraging at close. Credit improvement comes from extending the maturity to August 2032, scheduled amortisation and the excess-cash-flow sweep.

## 4. Reconstructed opening debt stack

The model uses the following post-refinancing GBP-equivalent opening stack:

| Instrument | Opening principal | Maturity | Modelled cash rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Existing term loans | £1,650m | 2030 | 7.50% |
| 2031 secured notes | £930m equivalent | 2031 | 7.82% weighted |
| Proposed term-out | £900m | 2032 | 8.25% |
| RCF | £0m drawn / £1,000m committed | 2030 | 7.50% when drawn |

The total opening principal of £3.48bn reconciles to the company's public description of approximately £3.5bn gross debt. The reconstruction is illustrative because complete post-refinancing debt documents and period-end cash balances are not publicly available.

## 5. Forecast mechanics

Revenue is grown from the FY2024/25 reported base. EBITDA equals revenue multiplied by the relevant scenario margin. Cash flow before debt paydown is calculated as:

> EBITDA − cash interest − cash tax − capital expenditure − lease principal − change in working capital − exceptional cash costs

The model maintains a £300m minimum cash balance. Surplus cash is available for scheduled amortisation and the applicable cash sweep. A cash deficit is funded by the RCF, subject to the £1.0bn commitment.

Cash interest uses average opening and closing debt balances where appropriate. Interest on the opening stack is therefore not understated simply because amortisation or sweep occurs later in the year. Lease interest is included in the coverage denominator because store leases are unavoidable fixed charges.

## 6. Cases

The base case assumes modest nominal sales growth and gradual margin improvement as cost savings offset inflation. The downside assumes revenue stagnation followed by a one-year decline, a margin trough of 4.65%, more conservative working capital and only partial capital-expenditure flexibility.

The key lender outputs are cash flow before paydown, RCF utilisation, liquidity, gross and net debt, net leverage and EBITDA / cash interest.

## 7. Proposed structure

The credit recommendation assumes:

- SONIA +450 bps, 50 bps floor and 99 original issue discount;
- 1% annual scheduled amortisation;
- a 50% excess-cash-flow sweep until net leverage is below 3.25x, falling to 25% until 2.75x and zero thereafter;
- £300m minimum cash and £500m minimum cash plus undrawn committed RCF;
- a 5.25x first-lien springing leverage covenant when more than 40% of the RCF is drawn;
- restricted-payment controls and at least 80% guarantor EBITDA coverage.

## 8. Limitations

This exercise does not include monthly seasonality, a full working-capital build, covenant EBITDA add-backs, legal recovery analysis, store-level capital expenditure, tax-group detail, hedging schedules or non-public debt documentation. It is deliberately transparent about those limitations and should be treated as an analytical portfolio project only.
